How do I play?

Clear every tile that isn’t a mine. A number tells you how many of its eight neighbours are mines. Tap to reveal and long-press to flag (you can swap these in Settings). Tap a number whose mines are all flagged to open its other neighbours at once. Home → How to Play has a visual guide.

Why must the Daily start on the highlighted tile?

Everyone gets the same Daily board, and it can be solved with logic alone from that tile. Starting there guarantees a fair, safe opening.

Did my streak reset?

Your streak counts consecutive days with a completed Daily, using your device’s local date. It stays alive until the end of the day after your last completion.

How do I restore Sweep Pro?

Open Settings → Sweep Pro → Restore Purchases while signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you bought it with.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Sweep is local-first with no accounts, so games, history and stats stay on the device. Sweep Pro restores on any device signed in to the same store account.
