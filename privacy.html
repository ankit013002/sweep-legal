Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026 · Petalform LLC

The short version

Sweep is local-first. You never create an account, and we never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts or location.

What stays on your device

Your games, Daily history, streaks, statistics and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

If you buy Sweep Pro, the App Store or Google Play processes the payment. We use RevenueCat to confirm your purchase using an anonymous app user ID. We never see your payment details.

Notifications

Daily reminders are optional and scheduled locally on your device. No push tokens are collected.

Analytics

Sweep may record anonymous, aggregate product events (such as “Daily completed”) to improve the game. These events contain no personal information and no individual taps.

Advertising

Sweep contains no ads and no advertising SDKs, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Sharing

When you share a result, Sweep hands a text or image to your device’s share sheet. Nothing is uploaded by Sweep.

Children

Sweep is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Sweep is published by Petalform LLC, Upper Sandusky, Ohio, USA. For questions, email petalformllc@gmail.com or visit Sweep Support.
